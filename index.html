Quick answers

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. Booking on WhatsApp skips the wait, especially on weekends.

How long does a haircut take?

Around 30 to 40 minutes. A haircut with beard styling is about an hour.

Is this a unisex salon?

Yes, everyone is welcome. Boys’ cuts, baby cuts and bridal makeup (male and female) are on the price list.

Which day are you closed?

The last Tuesday of every month is our holiday.
