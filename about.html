Our story

Welcome to Vinod Hair Lines, a trusted men’s grooming and hairstyling destination in the heart of Coonoor, proudly serving generations of customers since 1980.

With over four decades of experience, we combine traditional barbering expertise with modern hairstyling trends. Our commitment to quality, professional service and personalised attention ensures that every customer receives an exceptional grooming experience.

Great style begins with confidence. Whether you prefer a classic traditional haircut or a contemporary hairstyle, our experienced team is dedicated to helping you look your best. Owner: Vinod Raguram.
