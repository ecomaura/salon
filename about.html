Our story

Vinod Hair Lines is one of Coonoor’s traditional men’s grooming destinations, with a proud legacy dating back to 1980. In the heart of the town, we combine more than four decades of traditional barbering experience with modern hairstyling techniques.

Our focus is professional service, personalised attention, customer satisfaction and a comfortable, welcoming atmosphere. Owner: Vinod Raguram.
